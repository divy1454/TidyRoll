Tidyroll

Runs on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

Tidy your camera roll into albums.

Tidyroll looks at every photo and video in a folder and files copies into albums: trips, places, people, pets, food, screenshots and more. It all happens on this device, and your originals are never moved, changed or deleted.

You can also drop a folder anywhere on this page.

Phone browsers hide where photos were taken, so trips and places need the Tidyroll app for Android. Everything else works here.

How do I get my phone's photos onto this PC?
  1. Connect your phone with a USB cable and unlock it. If it asks, choose File transfer.
  2. In File Explorer, open the phone, then Internal storage, then DCIM.
  3. Copy the Camera and Screenshots folders into your Pictures folder.
  4. Come back here and choose that folder. Photos synced with OneDrive or Google Photos downloads work too.
